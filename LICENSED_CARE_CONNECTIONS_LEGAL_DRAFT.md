# Licensed Care Connections
## Draft Terms, Privacy Disclosure, and Feature Disclaimer

> **Draft — attorney review required before publication or launch.** This U.S.-oriented draft assumes that The Patient’s Compass remains an educational and navigation platform; does not employ, control, or practice medicine through clinicians; and initially uses a no-PHI-by-default handoff to independently operated telehealth partners. Counsel should confirm the company’s legal entity name, address, governing state, partner contracts, state-specific consumer-health requirements, and whether any actual data flow makes the company a HIPAA business associate.

## 1. Terms of Service Addendum

### Licensed Care Connections

Licensed Care Connections is an optional navigation feature that may help users identify or connect with independent, third-party healthcare professionals or telehealth services. The Patient’s Compass provides educational information and connection tools only. The Patient’s Compass is not a medical practice, healthcare provider, health plan, or emergency service and does not itself diagnose, treat, prescribe, or deliver telehealth care through this feature.

### Independent Providers and Partners

Healthcare professionals and telehealth organizations accessible through Licensed Care Connections are independent third parties. They are not employees, agents, partners, or representatives of The Patient’s Compass unless a separate written notice expressly states otherwise. Each provider or partner is solely responsible for professional licensure, scope of practice, clinical decisions, informed consent, medical records, billing, insurance participation, availability, continuity of care, malpractice coverage, and compliance with laws applicable to the care they provide.

The Patient’s Compass may review certain credentials or partner representations for program eligibility, but does not guarantee a provider’s qualifications, availability, suitability, outcomes, or continued licensure. Users should independently confirm a provider’s current license, location eligibility, accepted insurance, fees, and services before receiving care.

### No Provider-Patient Relationship With The Patient’s Compass

Use of Licensed Care Connections does not create a provider-patient relationship between the user and The Patient’s Compass. A provider-patient relationship, if any, is formed only between the user and the independent provider under that provider’s terms, consent forms, privacy practices, and professional obligations.

### Third-Party Terms and Payments

When a user leaves The Patient’s Compass or enters a partner-operated experience, the partner’s terms of service, privacy notice, telehealth consent, cancellation policy, billing terms, and clinical policies apply. Unless expressly stated otherwise, appointments, clinical fees, insurance claims, refunds, prescriptions, and disputes concerning clinical care are handled by the independent provider or telehealth partner—not by The Patient’s Compass.

### Location, Eligibility, and Consent

Telehealth eligibility depends on the user’s physical location at the time of care and the provider’s authority to practice in that jurisdiction. Users agree to provide accurate location and identity information to the treating provider and to complete any required telehealth informed consent. The Patient’s Compass does not guarantee that a particular provider or service is legally available in every location.

### Information Shared at the User’s Direction

The Patient’s Compass will not transmit health information to a Licensed Care Connections partner by default. If a future workflow allows a user to share information, the interface will identify the recipient, categories of information, purpose, and whether sharing is optional before the user authorizes the transfer. Users should review the partner’s privacy practices before sending information. Authorization to share information may not be revocable after the recipient has already received and lawfully used it.

### No Emergency Services

Licensed Care Connections is not designed for emergencies, urgent conditions, crisis response, or continuous monitoring. If a user believes they may have a medical emergency, they should call 911 or their local emergency number immediately. Users experiencing a mental-health crisis in the United States may call or text 988. Users should not wait for a platform message, referral, or appointment.

### No Warranty of Access or Outcome

Partner participation, appointment availability, service areas, and integrations may change. The Patient’s Compass does not warrant that a connection will be completed, that a provider will accept a user, or that any particular result will follow from third-party services.

### Complaints

Questions about Licensed Care Connections navigation may be sent to support@thepatientscompass.com. Questions or complaints about clinical care, medical records, provider conduct, billing, or telehealth consent should be directed to the responsible provider or partner and, when appropriate, the relevant professional licensing board.

## 2. Privacy Policy Addendum

### Licensed Care Connections Data

If Licensed Care Connections becomes available, The Patient’s Compass may process limited information needed to display eligible partner options or initiate a user-requested connection. Depending on the implemented workflow, this may include account identifiers, contact information, the user’s state or jurisdiction, selected service category, selected partner, referral timestamp, and records of disclosures or authorizations presented to the user.

The recommended initial implementation does **not** send lab results, uploaded documents, symptoms, diagnoses, medications, AI conversations, biomarker history, or other health content to a partner. Users should enter clinical information directly in the partner-operated environment after reviewing that partner’s privacy notice and consent forms.

### User-Directed Data Transfers

If a future version supports transferring health information, The Patient’s Compass will request a separate, clear authorization before transfer. The authorization should identify:

* the recipient;
* the specific data categories;
* the purpose of the transfer;
* whether the transfer is required or optional;
* the authorization’s duration;
* how to revoke future transfers; and
* the privacy notice governing the recipient’s subsequent use.

The Patient’s Compass will apply data minimization and will not place health information in URLs, referral codes, advertising pixels, or general-purpose analytics events.

### Independent Partner Privacy Practices

Independent providers and telehealth partners process information under their own privacy notices and, where applicable, HIPAA Notices of Privacy Practices. Once information is submitted directly to a partner—or transferred at the user’s direction—the partner’s legal obligations and policies govern its clinical records and subsequent use. The Patient’s Compass is not responsible for a partner’s independent privacy practices, but should contractually require appropriate security, lawful use, and incident cooperation.

### HIPAA and Consumer Health Data

The Patient’s Compass is an educational platform and is not itself a healthcare provider. Its general educational services may fall outside HIPAA. If The Patient’s Compass performs a service for a HIPAA-covered partner that involves creating, receiving, maintaining, or transmitting protected health information, it will treat that information according to the applicable Business Associate Agreement and HIPAA requirements. Other health-related information may be protected by the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and state consumer-health privacy laws even when HIPAA does not apply.[1][2]

### Retention and Deletion

Referral records should be retained only for the period necessary to complete the connection, satisfy security and contractual requirements, resolve support matters, and comply with law. User-directed transfer authorizations and required audit evidence may be retained for the applicable legal period. Clinical records maintained by an independent provider are subject to that provider’s retention duties. Users may contact privacy@thepatientscompass.com regarding Patient’s Compass records and should contact the provider regarding provider-held clinical records.

### Security and Incidents

Where health information is handled, The Patient’s Compass will use role-based access, authentication, encryption in transit and at rest, audit logging, integrity controls, documented incident response, and vendor oversight appropriate to the data and legal relationship. If an incident triggers a notification duty, The Patient’s Compass and its partners will follow the allocation and timelines required by applicable law and contract.[3][4]

> Connect with independent, licensed healthcare professionals through secure telehealth partners. Availability and services vary by location. Patients Compass provides education and navigation—not diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care.

The partner—not The Patient’s Compass—provides any clinical care and controls appointment availability, medical records, consent, billing, and treatment decisions. **Required acknowledgement:**

> I understand that the provider and telehealth service are independent from The Patient’s Compass, that this connection is not emergency care, and that the partner’s terms and privacy practices will apply.
