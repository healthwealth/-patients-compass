
## Subscription app setup references added 2026-10-09

- Shopify official setup guide: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/purchase-options/subscriptions/setup
  - Install/configure a subscription app first.
  - Then go to Products, open the product, and choose subscription-only or subscription plus one-time purchase.
  - Shopify adds a subscription cancellation policy when subscriptions are configured.
- Shopify Subscriptions app listing: https://apps.shopify.com/shopify-subscriptions
  - First-party Shopify app; listing currently shows Free.
  - Supports auto-billed weekly, monthly, or yearly subscriptions, discounts, pause/skip/cancel, reporting, and customer subscription management.
- Recharge app listing: https://apps.shopify.com/subscription-payments
  - Third-party subscription platform with customer portal, churn prevention, failed-payment recovery, bundles, loyalty, and analytics.
  - Listing currently shows plans from $25/month and a free trial; verify current commercial terms in Shopify before installation.
